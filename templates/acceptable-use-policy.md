# Acceptable Use Policy for AI Tools

**Company:** [COMPANY NAME]
**Effective date:** [DATE]
**Approved by:** [APPROVER NAME AND ROLE]
**Review date:** [REVIEW DATE]

## Purpose

This policy explains how staff at [COMPANY NAME] may use AI tools, models, and agents at work. It keeps our work fast and useful while protecting our customers, our data, and our reputation.

## What you may use AI for

You may use approved AI tools to:

- Draft emails, documents, summaries, and internal notes
- Brainstorm ideas and outline plans
- Write and review code, with a human checking the result
- Summarize meetings and public research
- Answer routine customer questions, using only the approved knowledge sources

Use your judgment. If a task involves a decision that affects a customer, money, or safety, a person makes the final call.

## What you must never paste into AI tools

Never put any of the following into an AI tool, chat, prompt, or agent:

- Customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details
- Passwords, API keys, tokens, or other credentials
- Payment details or financial records
- Health information or other sensitive personal data
- Confidential business plans, contracts, or unreleased product details
- Anything marked confidential or restricted

If you are unsure whether something is safe to share, treat it as unsafe and ask first.

## Agents and production systems

An agent is software that can take actions on its own, such as calling tools, sending messages, or changing data. Before any agent writes to a production system, a database, a customer account, or a live service, it needs approval from [APPROVER NAME AND ROLE]. Until then, agents run in read only mode.

No agent may deploy code, delete data, or contact a customer without a named human owner and a written approval on file.

## Approved tools

Only use AI tools listed in the team AI inventory. If you want to try a new tool, add it to the inventory as a trial and get approval from [APPROVER NAME AND ROLE] before using it with any work data.

## Who to ask

Questions about this policy, or about whether a use is allowed, go to [CONTACT NAME AND EMAIL]. If you think data was shared by mistake, report it to the same contact right away. Reporting a mistake quickly is always the right move, and nobody will be punished for an honest report.

## Review

This policy is reviewed on [REVIEW DATE], or sooner if we adopt a new tool or a rule changes.
