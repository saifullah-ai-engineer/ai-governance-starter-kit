AI Governance Starter Kit

Cost, Choice, Control

Good AI governance comes down to three questions. Keep them in balance, and small teams can use AI agents safely without slowing down.

PILLAR 1

Cost

Know what each model call costs, who is spending it, and where it goes.

PILLAR 2

Choice

No single model is best for every job. Pick a small approved set and test before you switch.

Which model fits this job?
PILLAR 3

Control

Know what AI you have, who can use it, and what it is allowed to do.
