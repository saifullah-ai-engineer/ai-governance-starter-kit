30-Minute Quick Start

30MINUTES

Ten small steps. One owner, one inventory, one weekly review.

1

Pick one owner

Read the five principles and name one person for AI governance.

2

List what you have

Fill in the AI inventory: every model, agent, tool, and data source.

3

Run the demo

See the tools work end to end: python tools/demo.py

4

Price one real task

Use the cost estimator and write the result next to that agent.

5

Add your top 3 risks

Open the risk register and give each risk an owner and a fix.

6

Share the policy

Edit the acceptable-use policy to fit your team, then share it.

7

Score your models

Use the rubric and record the winner in config, not in code.

8

Check your top agent

Walk the runtime checklist and fix the first gap you find.

9

Check the logs

Confirm the action logger records every agent action.

10

Test the policy

Run the policy checker on a sample action, then set a weekly review.

Done in 30 minutes. Repeat the review every week.
